# Content playbook — Dario Store

Words in [square brackets] are yours to fill in. Nothing here invents prices, delivery times or numbers: check
them against your real offer before posting.

## 1. Profile setup (Instagram + Facebook)

**Pictures:** `brand/profile-picture-yellow.png` (or cream / ink). Facebook cover: template "Facebook cover".

**Name:** Dario Store · **Username:** short, no underscores, the same on Instagram and Facebook.

**Bio (Instagram), pick a language or write both:**

```
Game accounts · Game keys · Coins · Subscriptions · IEMs 🇹🇳
Delivered by email after payment — D17, Binance Pay, bank, crypto
Order + track ↓
[your-site]
```

```
Comptes · Clés de jeu · Monnaie de jeu · Abonnements · IEMs
Livraison par e-mail après paiement — D17, Binance Pay, banque, crypto
Commander + suivre ↓
[votre-site]
```

(The flag is the only emoji we suggest; the brand look is emoji-free.)

**Link in bio:** the shop home page. For an AliExpress customer, send them their own tracking link in DMs.

**Highlights** (use the covers in `png/en/highlights` or `png/fr/highlights`): Games · Keys · Coins · Subs · IEMs · Reviews · Track · How.
Fill each with 3–6 stories: screenshots of the category on the site, prices, a few reviews, the "how to order" story.

**Pinned posts (3):** How to order · Payment methods · Best review.

## 2. Weekly rhythm (adapt to your time)

| Day | Feed post | Story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Product spotlight (a best seller) | Product story + poll "which game next?" |
| Tue | How to order or Payment methods | Question box |
| Wed | Customer review | Review story (with the customer's OK) |
| Thu | Promo or Price list | Flash sale story |
| Fri | Product spotlight (new / back in stock) | Announcement story |
| Sat | — | Behind the scenes: packing IEMs, a delivered order |
| Sun | AliExpress service | Order tracking story sent to a real customer (blur or mask personal details) |

Minimum plan when busy: **3 feed posts + 1 story a day**. Consistency beats volume.
Post when your buyers are online: check Insights → "Most active times" after two weeks and move posts there.

- FR: `Merci [nom masqué] ★★★★★ Vos avis sont la raison pour laquelle le chat reste ouvert sur chaque commande.`

Always end with one call to action (DM us / link in bio / tap the link sticker).

## 6. Before you post — checklist

- [ ] Handle and website in the studio are yours.
- [ ] Price, discount and end date are correct and match the website.
- [ ] No customer full name, phone, address, email or order number visible (blur or mask; ask before sharing a review).
- [ ] Never show your D17 number, RIB or crypto address in a public post; those appear only on the customer's order page.
- [ ] No promise you can't keep ("instant" only if the order is really sent right away after payment).
- [ ] Caption has one clear call to action.
- [ ] Story text is inside the safe area (not in the top/bottom 250 px).

## 7. What to look at each week

Reach and saves per post (which template works), story replies, DMs that became orders, profile visits → link
clicks. Repeat what brings orders, drop what doesn't. Note it next to each post's template name so you can compare.
